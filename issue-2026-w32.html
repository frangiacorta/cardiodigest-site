Tier 1 — Practice-changing

— Tirzepatide and the risk of atherosclerotic cardiovascular events: population based cohort study
Krüger N et al. · BMJ 2026 · source ↗

This population-based cohort study estimated the real-world reduction in major adverse cardiovascular events (MACE) from adding tirzepatide to standard of care. At one year, the weighted risk of MACE was 2.9% (95% confidence interval (CI) 2.5% to 3.4%) with tirzepatide versus 4.4% (3.8% to 4.9%) with sitagliptin — a risk difference of -1.4% (95% CI -2.1% to -0.7%; number needed to treat (NNT)=70) and a hazard ratio of 0.68 (95% CI 0.58 to 0.80).

— Duration of Therapeutic Hypothermia After Out-of-Hospital Cardiac Arrest: The ICECAP Randomized Clinical Trial
Meurer WJ et al. · JAMA 2026 · source ↗

Therapeutic hypothermia is widely used for neuroprotection after cardiac arrest, but trials have not consistently demonstrated improved neurological outcomes, and the optimal cooling duration remains uncertain. This trial used adaptive randomized allocation to therapeutic hypothermia at 33 °C for cooling durations of 6, 12, 18, 24, 30, 36, 42, 48, 60, or 72 hours to help settle the question.

— [(177)Lu]Lu-PSMA-617 in patients with PSMA-positive metastatic androgen pathway modulator-naive/sensitive prostate cancer (PSMAddition): a phase 3 randomised, controlled trial
Tagawa ST et al. · Lancet 2026 · source ↗

The contemporary standard of care for metastatic androgen pathway modulator-naive/sensitive (APMN/S) prostate cancer — also known as metastatic hormone-sensitive prostate cancer — is androgen deprivation therapy (ADT) plus an androgen receptor pathway inhibitor (ARPI) until progression. Between June 15, 2021, and July 25, 2023, 1529 patients were screened and 1144 randomly assigned (n=572 per arm; 1144 [100%] male, 572 [50%] with de novo metastatic APMN/S prostate cancer, 779 [68%] with high-volume disease; median age 68·0 years [IQR 62·0-73·0]).

— Iron or Multiple Micronutrient Powder Supplements With Malaria Chemoprevention in Rural Malawian Children: The IRMA Randomized Clinical Trial
Phiri KS et al. · JAMA 2026 · source ↗

The World Health Organization recommends universal preventive iron interventions for young children in anemia-prevalent areas to counter anemia's effects on child neurodevelopment and other outcomes. This trial tested six months of one of four regimens: iron syrup and malaria chemoprevention (n = 542), iron-containing micronutrient powders and malaria chemoprevention (n = 543), malaria chemoprevention alone (n = 541), or placebo alone (n = 542).

— Addition of High-Dose Vitamin D3 to Standard Treatment in Patients With Metastatic Colorectal Cancer: The SOLARIS Randomized Clinical Trial (Alliance A021703)
Ng K et al. · JAMA 2026 · source ↗

A phase 2 randomized clinical trial previously found that high-dose vitamin D3 added to standard treatment improved progression-free survival (PFS) compared with standard-dose vitamin D3 in patients with metastatic colorectal cancer (mCRC). In this trial of 455 randomized patients (median age, 59 years; 181 [40%] female) with median follow-up 20 months, the median PFS for high-dose vitamin D3 (n = 228) was 11.8 months (95% CI, 10.3-13.3) versus 10.3 months (95% CI, 9.4-12.2) for standard-dose vitamin D3 (n = 227) — a difference that did not reach significance (1-sided log-rank P = .25).

— Comparative effectiveness of azithromycin versus roflumilast in patients with chronic obstructive pulmonary disease: emulated target trial
Portela GT et al. · BMJ 2026 · source ↗

This emulated target trial compared the risk of moderate or severe exacerbations of chronic obstructive pulmonary disease (COPD) among new users of azithromycin versus roflumilast in a population of patients with active COPD treated in routine clinical practice. Azithromycin was associated with a 17% reduction, versus roflumilast, in the hazard of first moderate or severe COPD exacerbation (hazard ratio 0.83, 95% confidence interval (CI) 0.79 to 0.87; number needed to treat 15, 95% CI 12 to 21).
